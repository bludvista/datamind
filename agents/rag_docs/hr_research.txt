Employee attrition is heavily influenced by overtime work.
Studies show employees working overtime are 3x more likely to leave the organization.
Sales departments typically face higher attrition due to high pressure targets and commission-based stress.
Younger employees aged 25-35 are more likely to switch jobs frequently due to career growth opportunities.
Key retention strategies include flexible working hours, competitive pay raises,
career development programs, mentorship opportunities and regular manager feedback sessions.
Human Resources departments face attrition due to emotional burnout from handling employee issues.
Organizations with structured onboarding programs see 50% lower attrition in the first year.
Monthly income is a strong predictor of attrition — employees earning below market rate leave faster.
